# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

## Scope

This application is operated by a single individual for the sole purpose of
retrieving that individual's own health data from services where he holds an
account. There are no other users.

## Data collected

Health and wellness data belonging to the operator, retrieved via the
authenticated APIs of services he has personally authorized — including sleep,
readiness, activity, heart rate, heart rate variability, and related metrics.

## How data is used

Data is used solely for the operator's personal review and analysis.

## Storage

Data is stored locally on private hardware owned and physically controlled by
the operator. It is not stored on third-party servers or cloud services.

## Sharing

No data is sold, shared, transmitted, or disclosed to any third party.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained at the operator's discretion and may be deleted at any time.
Authorization can be revoked at any time through the source provider's own
account settings, which immediately ends this application's access.
